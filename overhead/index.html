✈️ A320 OVERHEAD PANEL TRAINER

Complete Flight Deck System Reference

1

Circuit Breaker Panel

Circuit Breaker Panel: The top section displays all electrical circuit breakers for the aircraft systems. Each breaker protects a specific aircraft system from electrical overcurrent. Green indicators show armed/normal status, and black indicators show tripped/fault conditions. This is the primary electrical protection system for all aircraft electronics and equipment.
Key Points: Familiarize yourself with common breaker trips, understand which systems are protected by each breaker section, and learn the proper procedures for resetting tripped breakers.
2

Fire Suppression System

Fire Suppression Panel: Located at the bottom of the overhead, this critical safety system allows flight crew to detect and suppress fires in three locations: Engine 1, APU, and Engine 2. The large red FIRE PUSH buttons discharge fire suppressant (Halon 1301) into the affected engine compartment. SQUIB DISCH and TEST buttons are used for system verification and maintenance.
Key Points: These are safety-critical controls - understand the proper sequence for fire detection and suppression, know the fire detection system logic, and memorize the emergency procedures for engine fires.
3

Electrical System Architecture

Electrical System Diagram: Shows the complete electrical power distribution network, including AC buses, DC buses, batteries, generators, and backup power sources. The green lines represent electrical connections and power flow paths. This schematic helps understand how electrical power is generated, distributed, and managed throughout the aircraft.
Key Points: Understand DC vs AC power distribution, battery management, generator operation and redundancy, and electrical bus priorities during system failures.
4

Main Overhead Controls Panel

Main Overhead Controls: The central section contains all major flight control systems including anti-ice systems, pneumatic distribution, hydraulic systems, air conditioning, pressurization, and lighting controls. Multiple colored indicators show system status (green = normal, amber/red = caution/warning). This panel represents the core of the aircraft's environmental and mechanical systems.
Key Points: Master all system controls, understand system interactions and dependencies, learn normal operating procedures and emergency configurations, and practice system management under various flight conditions.
